**Reviewer scope.** Independent re-review of the 004 fix-pass against
`REVIEW_schema_004_postgres.md` (2 BLOCKER, 8 SHOULD-FIX, 6 NIT) and
`REVIEW_schema_004_evaluation.md` (9 BLOCKER, 6 SHOULD-FIX, 8 NIT), and against

**Method.** Every disposition below was re-tested against a real PostgreSQL 16, not read off the

ran in a throwaway database `rh_review_scratch` created on `rh-db`, loaded with 001–004 and
**dropped afterwards**; the remaining probes ran against `rh-db` inside rolled-back transactions.
Seven regression tests were re-proven RED on a scratch copy of `db/` outside the repo. Final live
state verified: `schema_migrations` = 4 rows, checksums `ok`, **0 rows in every other table**, no
`km-*` container/volume/network touched, `data/market/` untouched, `git status` unchanged except
this report.

## Summary verdict

**PASS WITH CONDITIONS.**

10 of 11 blockers are genuinely closed — I reconstructed each original reviewer's failing case and
every one is now rejected by a named constraint, trigger, FK, or grant. The eleventh (EV B9) is
**PARTIALLY-FIXED**: the structural blocker is gone (a persona can now hold a standing book), but
"comparable" is still not enforced, and the migration comment that asserts it is **false**. All 14
should-fixes hold. Down migration fully reverses with zero residue; 001–003 behaviour is unchanged.
All four gates green.

The conditions are three **new untrue claims** — the project's signature defect, which the fix-pass
claimed to have killed and did not. Two of them the fix-pass *created*: it deleted the
`-- migrate:` directives from 001–003 and left both `db/migrate.py` and `ADR-002` asserting those
directives are still there, and asserting the very rule the fix-pass broke ("editing an applied
migration violates the checksum invariant"). Plus one operational finding: `ck_prd_mark_window`'s
hard-coded 4-day grace makes a legitimately late backfill **permanently unstorable**, with no
tuning path — the shape Bar §7.2 exists to prevent.

## New findings

### BLOCKER

**BLOCKER-1 — the fix-pass reintroduced the exact defect it claims to have killed, twice, in the
files that document its own rules.**
The fix-pass deleted `-- migrate: non-destructive` from `001`, `002`, and `003`. Two documents
still assert those lines are present, and assert the rule that forbade the edit:

- `db/migrate.py:23-24` — "the ones still present in the applied 001-003 up bodies stay only
  because editing an applied migration would break its checksum."
- `docs/adr/ADR-002-destructiveness-from-filename.md:79-82` — same claim, plus "editing an applied
  migration violates the checksum invariant."

Both are now false statements of fact about the repository, sitting in the runner docstring and the
ADR that a future reader will treat as authoritative. Worse, the fix-pass *reversed a recorded
architectural decision* (keep the inert lines rather than edit applied files) as a drive-by, and
recorded the reversal only in a fix report — the ADR that owns the decision was never amended. This
is the same failure mode as the compose file, the ADR, the docstring, and the "append-only" table
COMMENT that preceded it. Fix: delete both paragraphs or rewrite them to say the lines were removed
during the 004 fix-pass with all four checksums re-recorded, and add a Consequences bullet to
ADR-002.

*(Graded BLOCKER because the project's standing defect is untrue claims in authoritative docs, and
because a reader of ADR-002 today would conclude editing an applied migration is forbidden while
the tree shows three of them edited.)*

### SHOULD-FIX

**SHOULD-FIX-1 — `ck_prd_mark_window`'s hard-coded 4-day grace makes a late backfill permanently
unstorable, with no tuning path** (`004:608-611`). Verified: a Friday-2026-07-03 mark priced
2026-07-07 21:00Z (a plausible outage over a weekend plus the Monday holiday) is **rejected**; the
same mark priced Monday is accepted. Because the constraint is on `priced_as_of`, the rejection is
permanent — once the window has passed, that trading day can *never* be marked, so the series
silently acquires a gap. `trg_evaluation_runs_n` will then happily certify the reduced
`n_observations` as truthful, and `market_calendar`'s gap audit is deferred (S6), so nothing
