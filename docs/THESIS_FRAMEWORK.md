# Forward Thesis Framework — the forward-thinking layer

> Built June 3, 2026. This is the standing forward layer of the agentic loop (charter §4, the
> "Wasden lens" step), added because the screen + news are backward-looking. Wasden's real edge
> is forward: *"markets move on expectations"* and the top-down 5-bucket flow. This framework
> forces every candidate through a forward, ecosystem-grounded thesis BEFORE any trade.

## Two passes, top-down then bottom-up (Wasden's actual order)

### Pass 1 — Top-down (where should we even be?)
Before picking stocks, answer the bucket questions for the next 1–3 months:
- **Countries:** rates / inflation / currency — which markets are favored?
- **Sectors:** where is capital rotating *to*?
- **Themes (bucket 4, structural, multi-year):** AI/compute, aging demographics, energy/electrification, reshoring, etc. — which are accelerating vs. fading?
- **Special situations (bucket 5):** where is the market *absolutely wrong* right now, before the crowd sees it?

Output: a short ranked list of themes/sectors to *overweight* and *avoid* this cycle. Screen *within* the favored ones.

### Pass 2 — Bottom-up thesis (per company / group)
Every candidate gets a written thesis in the structure below. A thesis is **forward and falsifiable** — not "it's cheap and had good news," but "X will happen by date E because A/B/C, and I'm wrong if D."

## Thesis structure (the standing template)

1. **Core thesis** — 1–2 sentences: the forward claim, the mechanism, the horizon.
2. **Setup (the now)** — price, key ratios (PEG/FCF yield/Piotroski), cheap-or-expensive, the decisive recent news.
3. **Forward drivers (ecosystem, projected forward)** — what's likely *next* across:
   - **Economy / macro** — rates, inflation, growth, currency, capital flows
   - **Society / social trends** — demand shifts, demographics, behavior change
   - **The business** — products, margins, capacity, pipeline, capital allocation
   - **Supply chain** — upstream dependencies, bottlenecks, single points of failure
   - **Competitors** — share shifts, new entrants, disruption threats
   - **Customers / clients** — concentration, demand durability, who buys next
   - **Management / CEOs** — track record, capital allocation, succession
   - **Innovation / discoveries** — tech shifts, R&D, breakthroughs
   - **Governments / regulation** — policy, subsidies, antitrust, pricing
   - **Geopolitics** — trade, tariffs, conflict, supply security
4. **Expectations gap** — what the market *expects* vs. what we think happens. **This gap is the edge.**
5. **Catalysts** — near-term (dated) + medium-term + **non-obvious / second-order** (what the crowd hasn't repriced).
6. **Horizon** — the time frame for the thesis to play out.
7. **Falsification** — specific, dated-where-possible signals that prove the thesis wrong → exit.
8. **Conviction + role** — high/med/low; role in the slate (engine / anchor / payload / ballast / catalyst-rental).
9. **Entry / exit discipline** — levels, staging, stops, gap rules.

> Reactivity stays as the seatbelt (buy fear, exit into gaps). This layer adds the *anticipatory*
> dimension on top. The forward thesis sets WHAT and WHY; technical/catalyst timing still sets WHEN.

Living theses: `docs/THESES.md` (re-read and updated every cycle; falsified theses are exited and logged).
